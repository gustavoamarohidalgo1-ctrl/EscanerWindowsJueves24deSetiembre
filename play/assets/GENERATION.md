## Gráfico de funciones

El fondo original se generó con la herramienta integrada `imagegen` el 20 de agosto de 2026 y se
recortó de forma determinista a 1024 × 500 px. No contiene texto, logotipos, números, moneda ni
campos fiscales. Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Google Play feature graphic background for an Android
> business productivity app. Primary request: a polished, original wide illustration suggesting
> that a photographed purchase receipt becomes organized inventory. Scene/backdrop: clean light
> mint-to-white studio background with subtle depth. Subject: on the right half, one generic blank
> receipt page with abstract unreadable placeholder lines flowing into neatly organized product
> boxes and simple inventory cards; no people. Style/medium: premium modern 3D-flat hybrid
> illustration, restrained and professional, suitable for a small-business software product.
> Composition/framing: very wide landscape composition; reserve the entire left 45 percent as calm
> uncluttered negative space for later brand copy; focal illustration on the right; safe margins.
> Lighting/mood: soft daylight, trustworthy, efficient, calm. Color palette: deep emerald green
> #006C4C, mint, white, small muted teal accents. Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no
> logos, no trademarks, no currency symbols, no identifiable invoice fields, no watermark, no
> device frame, no rounded outer border. Avoid: photorealistic fiscal documents, clutter,
> exaggerated gradients, neon colors, stock-photo look.

Este prompt se conserva únicamente como procedencia del recurso histórico verde. No debe
reutilizarse para nuevos recursos: la identidad vigente usa azul cobalto/navy, neutros grafito
y acentos cobre.
